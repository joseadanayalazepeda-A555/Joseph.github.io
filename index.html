Current research direction · 2026

Human intelligence using technological tools

I am exploring what happens when a human researcher uses technology as a cognitive mirror while preserving periods of independent reflection. One emerging hypothesis is that when technology reaches a boundary, the resulting pause may create an opportunity for the human to continue the thinking process: connect prior information, generate a new hypothesis, and return to the technology with a better question.

Question
AI interaction
Boundary / pause
Independent reflection
New hypothesis
Return + refine

The scope is intentionally narrow: the researcher is the human being under study; technology is the instrument; the research environment is the context. Future versions must be earned through research rather than assumed in advance.
